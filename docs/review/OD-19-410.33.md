## 1. The question

> 42 CFR 410.33 has five transcribed criteria, all drawn from paragraphs (b), (c) and (d). Paragraph (a)(2) exempts four categories of diagnostic test from "the criteria set forth in paragraphs (b) through (e) and (g) and (h)" - that is, from every criterion transcribed here - and none of those four categories is itself transcribed. Paragraph (c)(1) is likewise expressly subject to an alternative in (c)(2), which is not transcribed either. How do these five criteria combine, and may a decision logic be declared for this policy version on the criteria that exist?

## 3. The transcribed criteria

These five are what the system can currently test. Everything else in the regulation is text the retriever can return but no criterion asserts.

| id | type | section | authoritative text |
|---|---|---|---|
| `C01` | REQUIRED | Ordering of tests | *"must be specifically ordered in writing by the physician who is treating the beneficiary"* |
| `C02` | REQUIRED | Ordering of tests | *"The order must specify the diagnosis or other basis for the testing"* |
| `C03` | REQUIRED | Supervising physician | *"must evidence proficiency in the performance and interpretation of each type of diagnostic procedure"* |
| `C04` | REQUIRED | Nonphysician personnel | *"must demonstrate the basic qualifications to perform the tests in question"* |
| `C05` | EXCLUSION | Supervising physician | *"must be limited to providing general supervision to no more than three IDTF sites"* |

**`C01`** — A verbal or standing order is insufficient; the order must be written and from the physician treating the specific problem.

**`C02`** — An order naming only the procedure, without the diagnosis or clinical basis, does not satisfy this.

**`C03`** — Documented by specialty certification, or by criteria the carrier accepts, for every procedure type the IDTF performs.

**`C04`** — Evidenced by State licensure or certification, or by a national credentialing body where no State board exists.

**`C05`** — Supervising a fourth concurrent site breaches the limit for all of them.

## 4. The provisions to read

Quoted verbatim from `data/cms/CFR-410_33-2026-08-13.md` (sha256 `dcf2a6ea050d…`, eCFR, revision 2026-08-13). Each is located in the source at build time, so this packet cannot drift from the regulation it cites.

### (a)(2) Exceptions - the wholesale exemption

> (2) Exceptions. The following diagnostic tests that are payable under the physician fee schedule and furnished by a nonhospital testing entity are not required to be furnished in accordance with the criteria set forth in paragraphs (b) through (e) and (g) and (h) of this section.

Exempts the listed categories from paragraphs (b) through (e), (g) and (h). Every transcribed criterion for this policy comes from (b), (c) or (d), so a case within (a)(2) is exempt from all of them.

### (a)(2)(i)-(iv) - the four exempt categories

> (i) Diagnostic mammography procedures, which are regulated by the Food and Drug Administration.
> (ii) Diagnostic tests personally furnished by a qualified audiologist as defined in section 1861(ll)(3) of the Act.
> (iii) Diagnostic psychological testing services personally furnished by a clinical psychologist or a qualified independent psychologist as defined in program instructions.
> (iv) Diagnostic tests (as established through program instructions) personally performed by a physical therapist who is certified by the American Board of Physical Therapy Specialties as a qualified electrophysiologic clinical specialist and permitted to provide the service under State law.

None of these four is transcribed as a criterion, so no evidence set can currently establish that a case falls inside the exemption.

### (b)(1) - the three-site supervision limit (source of C05)

> (b) Supervising physician. (1) Each supervising physician must be limited to providing general supervision to no more than three IDTF sites. This applies to both fixed sites and mobile units where three concurrent operations are capable of performing tests.

Transcribed as an EXCLUSION. It constrains the supervising physician across sites rather than the test being adjudicated.

### (c)(1) - nonphysician qualifications, expressly subject to (c)(2)

> (c) Nonphysician personnel. (1) Except as otherwise stated in paragraph (c)(2) of this section, any nonphysician personnel used by the IDTF to perform tests must demonstrate the basic qualifications to perform the tests in question and have training and proficiency as evidenced by licensure or certification by the appropriate State health or education department. In the absence of a State licensing board, the technician must be certified by an appropriate national credentialing body. The IDTF must maintain documentation available for review that these requirements are met.

Source of C04. Its opening words make it conditional on (c)(2).

### (c)(2) - the alternative route, not transcribed

> (2) For services that do not require direct or in-person beneficiary interaction, treatment, or testing, any nonphysician personnel used by the IDTF to perform the tests must meet all applicable State licensure requirements for doing so. If there are any applicable State licensure requirements, the IDTF must maintain documentation available for review that these requirements are met.

A different qualification standard for services without in-person interaction. Not transcribed, so C04 cannot currently be relieved by it.

## 5. The options

- `DECLARE_PLAIN_CONJUNCTION` — All four REQUIRED criteria must hold and the EXCLUSION denies. (a)(2) and (c)(2) are recorded as out of scope for this corpus.
- `DECLARE_WITH_A2_EXCEPTION` — Conjunction as above, wrapped in an exception: the whole requirement set is relieved when the case falls within any of (a)(2)(i)-(iv).
- `INSUFFICIENT_TRANSCRIPTION` — No logic may be declared on the criteria that exist. The provisions carrying this policy's structure - (a)(2) and (c)(2) - are absent, so any declaration would be a statement about a fragment.
- `OTHER` — Determined by the reviewer's stated reading.

## 6. What each one costs

### `DECLARE_PLAIN_CONJUNCTION`

Declaring this makes 42 CFR 410.33 admissible and it is the only option that does. It also means a diagnostic mammography procedure, or an audiologist-furnished test, would be adjudicated against criteria the regulation expressly exempts it from - the system would deny on a rule that does not apply to that case. Whether the corpus can contain such a case, and whether that risk is acceptable, is the reviewer's call.

### `DECLARE_WITH_A2_EXCEPTION`

Structurally correct against the text, and not expressible today - the four categories are not transcribed, so there is nothing for the exception to test. Requires transcription of (a)(2)(i)-(iv) first, after which the logic can be declared and the policy becomes admissible.

### `INSUFFICIENT_TRANSCRIPTION`

42 CFR 410.33 stays inadmissible and no policy version is available for the first vertical slice. Costs nothing and unblocks nothing; it says the transcription is not yet a basis for a decision rule, which may simply be true.

### `OTHER`

Deliberately not modelled. Precomputing a consequence for an unstated reading would mean inventing the reading.
