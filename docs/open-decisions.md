# Open Decisions

Genuinely unsettled questions. An item leaves this list only with the artefact or decision that
resolved it — not by being forgotten.

Settled decisions live in [adr/](adr/). If something here is decided, it becomes or amends an ADR
and the row is marked `Resolved` with a pointer.

| **OD-24** | Should the four `REVIEW_REQUIRED` policy versions be routed to `HUMAN_REVIEW` at runtime? | Opened by ADR-023. The logic inventory classifies 410.33, both 410.38 revisions and 411.15 as REVIEW_REQUIRED - they carry exception, alternative or conditional wording and nobody has established their logic. At runtime they still get an ASSUMED_CONJUNCTION, so **the record states a doubt the runtime does not act on** (R-59). Closing it means either declaring their logic, which requires reading their provisions (OD-19), or routing them to human review, which turns every gold_v1 case for those policies into HUMAN_REVIEW and forces gold_v2 - on the strength of a lexical signal scan. Neither was chosen, because picking the convenient one would be worse than recording the gap. | A completed OD-19 review, or an explicit decision to accept gold_v2 | — |
