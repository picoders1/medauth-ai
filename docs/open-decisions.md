| **OD-43** | **Who is the human in "human review", and how does the audit know?** The API key identifies the **integrating caller** - a system, not a person. A reviewer names themselves in the request body, and nothing verifies it. So `human_review_events.reviewer_id` records who *claimed* to decide, which is the one field in an otherwise-sound append-only trail that cannot be relied on after a bad outcome. **This must not be solved with a second API-key field**: a per-reviewer key is still a shared secret held by an integrating system, and it would make the trail look authenticated while changing nothing. What is required: an authenticated human identity distinct from the API integrator; a reviewer role and an authorization decision that is not merely case ownership; the verified identity written immutably into the audit event at the moment of decision; and attribution that distinguishes acceptance from override per person.
